Soundcore Pull

The Mac app that pulls recordings off your Soundcore Work AI voice recorder straight into iCloud Drive.

No phone. No account. No cloud service.

Get it on GitHub Free and open source. MIT licence. macOS 26 or later.

Get Soundcore Work recordings on to your Mac

🔁

Leave it open

The app looks for the recorder every 15 seconds. When it finds it, it copies every recording that is not already in iCloud Drive.

🎧

Lossless

Each file is an Ogg/Opus copy of exactly what the device stores. No transcoding. No quality loss.

🔒

Private

Audio goes from the recorder to your Mac over Bluetooth. Files land in iCloud Drive › Soundcore Work. Nothing else sees them.

How to copy files from a Soundcore Work recorder to a Mac

  1. Wake the recorder

    Take it out of the case or tap it. It does not advertise while it sleeps.

  2. Close the phone app

    The recorder talks to one device at a time. Soundcore Pull waits until the phone lets go.

  3. Watch the files appear

    Recordings show in the list as they download. Show in Finder opens the folder. Delete from Recorder frees space on the device.

Questions

Is there a Soundcore Work app for Mac?
Anker only ships a phone app. Soundcore Pull is a free, open source Mac app that copies recordings from the Soundcore Work recorder to your Mac.
Can I use the Soundcore Work recorder without the phone app?
Yes. Soundcore Pull talks to the recorder directly over Bluetooth LE. You do not need a phone, an account or a cloud service.
Does it work with the Anker D3200 or Feishu D3200?
Yes. Soundcore Work, Anker D3200 and Feishu D3200 are the same recorder.
What format are the files?
Ogg/Opus. Each file is a lossless copy of what the recorder stores. Files land in iCloud Drive, in a folder named Soundcore Work.
